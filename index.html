How the AI + UV Pipeline Works

Traditional print shops slap flat JPEGs onto glass, washing out shadows and killing dynamic range. Befargly treats glass as an optical medium—co-designing neural image synthesis with industrial UV-LED printhead physics.

01 STAGE I · VISION

Claude Vision Photo Analysis

Every uploaded frame is parsed by our fine-tuned Claude Vision orchestration layer to map semantic subjects, specular highlights, shadow depth, and color gamut boundaries before a single pixel is altered.

Semantic Depth Masking 99.4% Accuracy
02 STAGE II · 300 DPI

8K AI Upscaling

Glass reveals every compression artifact. Our diffusion-guided super-resolution engine reconstructs natural micro-textures—skin pores, brushed metal, oil brushwork—up to 18,000 pixels at true 300 DPI.

4x–8x Tensor Super-Res Zero Haloing
03 STAGE III · UV RIP

Automated CMYK+White RIP Channel

Instead of a flat white flood coat, Befargly computes a continuous 8-bit Spot-White UV channel. Highlights receive 100% opaque titanium white while midtones stay semi-translucent for 3D optical depth.

5-Channel TIFF Export ISO Coated v2
04 STAGE IV · CURING

Tempered Glass Curing

Printed directly onto the reverse surface of diamond-edged, thermally tempered low-iron glass and instantly cross-linked via 395nm UV-LED arrays—creating a waterproof, scratch-proof heirloom for 100+ years.

395nm UV-LED Crosslink 100-Yr Archival
